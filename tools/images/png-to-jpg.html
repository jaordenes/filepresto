Why Convert PNG to JPG?

JPG files are typically much smaller than PNG files, making them ideal for web use, email attachments, and storage. If your PNG doesn’t require transparency, converting it to JPG can save significant disk space without visible quality loss at high quality settings.

How It Works

Our converter uses the HTML5 Canvas API to read your PNG locally in your browser and encode it as a JPEG. No data is ever sent to a server.
