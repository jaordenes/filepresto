What is HEIC?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is Apple’s modern image format used by iPhones. While it offers excellent compression, it’s not universally supported. Converting to JPG ensures your photos work everywhere.

How It Works

We use the open-source heic2any library to decode HEIC files entirely in your browser. No files are ever uploaded to a server.
