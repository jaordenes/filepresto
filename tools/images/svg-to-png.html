Why Convert SVG to PNG?

SVG is a vector format that scales without quality loss, but not all applications support it. Converting to PNG creates a raster image compatible with all image viewers, email clients, and design tools.

How It Works

The SVG is loaded into an Image element and rendered onto an HTML5 Canvas at your specified dimensions. No data is ever sent to a server.
