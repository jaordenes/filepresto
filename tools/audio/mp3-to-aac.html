About MP3 to AAC Conversion

AAC (Advanced Audio Coding) is the default audio format for Apple devices and offers better sound quality than MP3 at similar bitrates. Converting MP3 to AAC ensures compatibility with iOS, macOS, and Apple TV. Processing happens client-side via WebAssembly - your file is never sent to any server.
